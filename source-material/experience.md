## 02. AI4ALL Ignite Scholar — Applied AI in Healthcare

**Organization:** AI4ALL  
**Dates:** 2025–2026  
**Project period:** August 2025 – November 2025  
**Type:** Applied AI education and project program  
**Portfolio emphasis:** Highest

### Description

Participated in AI4ALL Ignite, an applied artificial-intelligence program centered on technical development, responsible AI, collaboration, and real-world problem solving.

Collaborated on a pediatric EEG seizure-detection research project using the CHB-MIT Scalp EEG Database. Our team developed and evaluated a one-dimensional convolutional neural network for classifying seizure and non-seizure EEG windows.

### Responsibilities and Contributions

- Prepared and processed more than 9,500 multichannel EEG windows.
- Worked with inputs containing 18 EEG channels and 2,048 samples per window.
- Helped develop a 1D CNN for seizure-versus-non-seizure classification.
- Participated in model training, evaluation, interpretation, and documentation.
- Helped expose the model through a Streamlit research demonstration.
- Contributed to presenting the project’s methodology, results, and limitations.
- Applied responsible-AI principles to avoid presenting the model as a clinical diagnostic device.

### Technical Result

- Approximately 90% held-out test accuracy on the project’s prepared evaluation split.

This result represents research evaluation, not clinical validation.

### Recognition

The pediatric EEG project was selected for presentation at the AI4ALL Symposium.

### Technologies

- Python
- TensorFlow
- Keras
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Scikit-learn
- Google Colab
- Streamlit
- CHB-MIT Scalp EEG Database

### Recommended Short Portfolio Version

Collaborated on a pediatric EEG seizure-detection research project through AI4ALL Ignite. Our team processed more than 9,500 multichannel EEG windows and developed a 1D CNN that achieved approximately 90% held-out test accuracy. The project was selected for presentation at the AI4ALL Symposium.
